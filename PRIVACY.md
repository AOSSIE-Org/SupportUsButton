# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 1 November 2026

## Introduction

Support Us Button is committed to protecting your privacy and providing a transparent and user-friendly experience.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled by:

- the **Library**: the Support Us Button React component (npm `support-us-button`), and
- the **Website**: [the demo site](https://aossie-org.github.io/SupportUsButton/), where you can try the component.

Both follow a privacy-conscious and local-first approach. Neither has user accounts, cookies, analytics or advertising.

## Information and Permissions

**The Library** does not collect, store or send any information. It renders the sponsor tiers and links that the website owner configures. To match the host page's theme, it reads the page's font, background and text colours in the visitor's browser; that never leaves the browser.

**The Website** does not ask you for personal information and does not request any device permissions. It saves your demo settings in your browser's local storage, so the playground looks the same when you come back:

- `demo_project_name` and `demo_project_description`: the text you typed into the demo
- `demo_show_logo`, `demo_theme_prop`, `demo_host_bg_key` and `demo_host_font_key`: the display options you picked

This stays in your browser and is never sent to us.

## Data Storage

All information is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to or maintained on any remote server.

## Data Sharing

The Library and the Website do not sell your personal information, and do not use personal information for targeted advertising.

- **Sponsor links:** donation and sponsor links (such as GitHub Sponsors) take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.
- **Share button:** the Share button in the Website's footer opens the platform you choose (WhatsApp, X, LinkedIn, Telegram or your email app) with the page's address, or copies it. That platform's privacy policy applies; the Website does not record what you share.
- **Hosting:** the Website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives your IP address and standard request details when your browser loads the Website, and may keep them in server logs. See [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

Where a third party processes information, its handling of that information is governed by its own privacy policy and terms of service.

## Data Security

The Library and the Website aim to minimize privacy and security risks by not collecting data and by keeping demo settings on your device.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users are also responsible for maintaining the security of their devices.

## Data Deletion

You can remove everything the Website stores by clearing your browser's site data for aossie-org.github.io.

## Children's Privacy

The Library and the Website are not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children where such collection is prohibited by applicable law. They do not collect personal information from anyone.

## Free Access

The Library and the Website are free to use, with no paid features.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the Library, the Website, or applicable legal requirements.

Any updates will be made available wherever this Privacy Policy is published.
